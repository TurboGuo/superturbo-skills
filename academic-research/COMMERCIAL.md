# Commercial licensing

`academic-research` is published under the
[PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0](LICENSE).

## Free, no licence needed

- Your own dissertation, thesis, papers and grant applications
- Teaching and coursework
- Personal study, hobby projects, and evaluation
- Use by a university, charity, public research body or government institution,
  **whatever the source of its funding**

If you are a researcher using this on your own work, you are covered. Nothing to
sign, nothing to pay. That covers most people who will ever open this plugin.

## Requires a commercial licence

- Consulting or contract research delivered to a paying client
- Use inside a for-profit company's research, insights or policy function
- Reselling, hosting, or embedding this plugin in a paid product or service
- Training or workshops sold for a fee that run on this plugin

## Getting one

## Two things to know

**PolyForm Noncommercial is not an OSI-approved licence.** GitHub will show it
as "unrecognized" rather than naming it. That is expected, not a mistake.

**The noncommercial carve-out is deliberately wide.** Every university, school,
charity and public research body uses this for free regardless of how well
funded it is. For a plugin aimed at researchers that is most of the audience,
and it is meant to be — the licence exists to keep commercial redistribution a
conversation, not to charge students.
