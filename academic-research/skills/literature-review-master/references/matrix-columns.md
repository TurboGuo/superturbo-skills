# Synthesis matrix columns

Pick the columns that make the argument visible. More columns is not better;
the right columns are the ones where reading down reveals something.

## Core (always)
citekey · year · research question · theoretical frame · design · data and
sample · key finding · how it relates to my argument

## Quantitative projects
independent variable and its measure · dependent variable and its measure ·
controls · identification strategy · effect size and direction · sample size ·
statistical power reported · preregistered

## Qualitative projects
setting · participants and recruitment · data type · analytic approach ·
coding scheme origin (a priori or emergent) · saturation claim · reflexivity
statement · transferability claim

## Theory-building projects
construct definition used · level of analysis · mechanism proposed · boundary
conditions stated · propositions offered

## Systematic reviews and meta-analyses
inclusion decision and reason · risk-of-bias rating · effect size extracted ·
variance or standard error · moderators coded · reviewer 1 / reviewer 2 codes

## Comparative and historical
cases selected and why · case selection logic (most similar, most different,
deviant, typical) · time period · sources (archival, interview, secondary) ·
counterfactual considered

## Reading down the columns
- **One value repeated everywhere** → an unexamined assumption. The strongest
  kind of gap, because you can point at it.
- **Contradictory values** → a debate. Check the neighbouring columns:
  contradictions in social science usually come from different measures,
  different populations, or different levels of analysis, and naming which one
  is itself a contribution.
- **Mostly empty** → either a genuine hole or the wrong column. Ask which.
- **Values cluster by year** → the field changed. Say what changed it.
