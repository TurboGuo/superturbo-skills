# Literature review anti-patterns

## 1. The annotated bibliography in disguise
Every paragraph opens with an author name and closes with what they found.
Test: cover the citations. If the paragraph still says something, it is a
synthesis. If it collapses, it is a list.
Fix: write the claim first, then attach the sources that support it.

## 2. The assertion gap
"However, few studies have examined the relationship between X and Y."
Nobody believes this sentence, because everyone has written it.
Fix: point at the matrix. "Of the 23 studies in Table 1, all measure X at the
individual level; none measures it where the proposed mechanism operates."

## 3. Citation stuffing
Eleven sources in one parenthesis, none of which the author read past the
abstract. Reviewers spot it by checking whether the cited work actually says
what it is cited for.
Fix: cite the two or three that carry the claim, and say what each contributes.

## 4. The hedge mismatch
A cross-sectional survey cited for "X causes Y". This is the single fastest way
to lose a methodologist reviewer.
Fix: match the verb to the design. See insight-master's causal language ladder.

## 5. The dead theory section
A theory is introduced, defined, and never used again.
Fix: either the theory does work later in the paper, or it comes out.

## 6. Home-team bias
Only the author's own tradition is cited; the rival tradition is absent rather
than argued with. Absence reads as ignorance, not as disagreement.
Fix: cite the strongest version of the rival position, then say why it does not
account for the phenomenon.

## 7. Recency-only or classics-only
A review with nothing before 2019 has no foundation. A review with nothing after
2019 is out of date. Both are visible at a glance from the reference list.

## 8. The undifferentiated "researchers"
"Researchers have found..." Which researchers, working in which tradition, using
what evidence? The passive plural hides exactly the disagreement a review exists
to surface.

## 9. Silent scope creep
The review promises "trust in organizations" and delivers interpersonal trust,
institutional trust, and political trust as if they were one construct.
Fix: define the construct in the first 200 words and hold the boundary.

## 10. The gap that is not a gap
Nobody has studied X because X is uninteresting, unmeasurable, or already
answered under a different name. Before claiming a gap, search the other name.

## 11. 有述无评
The Chinese-language version of anti-pattern 1: 研究现状 runs for pages and
研究评述 is two sentences. The appraisal is the part reviewers read.

## 12. The translated-English 综述
Chinese academic register is not English syntax in Chinese words. Draft in the
target language rather than translating.
