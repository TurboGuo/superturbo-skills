# Paragraph patterns for a literature review

> **Every citation in this file is a placeholder in square brackets.** No real
> author, paper or finding appears in the worked examples, so nothing here can
> be copied into a reference list by accident. Substitute your own sources from
> `.research/library/`.
> 本文件中所有引注均为方括号占位符，不涉及任何真实文献。

## The synthesis paragraph (default)
```
CLAIM         Your sentence. Nobody's finding, your reading of the field.
EVIDENCE      2-5 sources grouped by what they share, cited together.
COMPLICATION  The source that does not fit, and why it does not.
WARRANT       What this pattern means for your argument.
```
Example:
> Network research has treated brokerage as a stable positional advantage
> **[claim]**. Studies across labour markets, R&D teams and civic associations
> find that actors spanning structural holes secure resources earlier and on
> better terms ([Source A]; [Source B]; [Source C]) **[evidence]**. Yet
> [Source D] finds the advantage disappears where ties are formally
> assigned rather than chosen, which suggests the mechanism is not position but
> the discretion position confers **[complication]**. If discretion is the
> mechanism, brokerage should predict outcomes only where actors control who
> they connect — a condition none of these studies varies **[warrant]**.

## The debate paragraph
```
POSITION A    with its strongest evidence
POSITION B    with its strongest evidence
THE CRUX      what the two sides actually disagree about, which is usually
              not what they say they disagree about
YOUR READING  which side the evidence favours, or why the debate is
              mis-specified
```

## The construct paragraph
```
DEFINITION    the definition you adopt, cited to its source
VARIANTS      how others define it differently, and what turns on the difference
YOUR BOUNDARY the version you use and why it fits the question
```
Put this early. A review that defines its central construct on page nine has
already used it eight times under an unstated meaning.

## The gap paragraph (the handoff)
```
SETTLED       what the field has established, in one sentence
UNSETTLED     what remains contested, in one sentence
ABSENT        what nobody has examined, pointed at in the matrix
THEREFORE     the present study, in one sentence
```
Never let ABSENT be a hope. It is a cell you can point to.

## Verb stance ladder
| Verb | Signals |
|---|---|
| demonstrates, shows | You accept the finding and the design supports it |
| finds, reports | Neutral, the standard default |
| argues, contends | A theoretical position, not an empirical result |
| claims, asserts | You have doubts and are flagging them politely |
| assumes, takes for granted | You are about to challenge it |
| suggests, indicates | The evidence is suggestive but not decisive |

## 中文综述段落示范 / The same paragraph in Chinese

同一个综合段落，用中文学术语体直接写，不是翻译。注意「表明」「假定」「与之相反」
承担的立场，以及最后一句把发现推回到本文的切入点。

> 网络研究长期把中介位置视为一种稳定的结构优势**【论点】**。在劳动力市场、研发
> 团队和社会组织三类情境中，跨越结构洞的行动者都能更早获得资源、并以更有利的条件
> 获得资源（［文献甲］；［文献乙］；［文献丙］）**【证据】**。然而，［文献丁］发现，
> 当关系由组织正式指派而非行动者自主选择时，这一优势随之消失，这提示真正起作用的
> 并非位置本身，而是位置所赋予的自主裁量空间**【张力】**。若机制确为裁量空间，那么
> 中介位置只应在行动者能够决定与谁连接的条件下预测结果——而上述研究均未对这一条件
> 加以变化**【推论】**。

三点值得注意：

1. **「提示」「表明」「说明」不是同义词。** 强度依次递增，`表明` 接近英文的
   `demonstrates`，横截面数据不要用。
2. **「然而」「与之相反」「值得注意的是」是评述部分的转折信号**，一段里最多一个，
   连用会稀释掉真正的分歧。
3. **中文引注里外文作者保留原名，中文作者用姓**，同一段里不要混用拼音和汉字。
4. **上面两段里的引注都是占位符。** 换成 `.research/library/` 里的真实文献之后，
   再逐条核对被归给每个来源的发现是否真的是那篇论文说的——把一个真实作者跟他没
   说过的结论绑在一起，比引一篇不存在的论文更难被发现，也更糟。

## Chinese register notes
- 学者们普遍认为 is the equivalent of "researchers have found" and carries the
  same anti-pattern 8 problem. Name the 学者 and the 学派.
- 值得注意的是 / 然而 / 与之相反 are the workhorse turn signals in 评述.
- Do not translate English hedging word for word. 可能 and 或许 are weaker than
  "may"; 表明 is stronger than "suggests".
