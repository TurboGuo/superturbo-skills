# Journal figure conventions

## Format
- **Vector for submission**: SVG, PDF or EPS. Raster only when the journal
  insists, and then at 300 dpi minimum (600 for line art at some presses).
- **Fonts embedded.** Arial, Helvetica or Times at 8 pt minimum after the figure
  is scaled to its printed column width. Check by printing at actual size.
- **Column width**: single column is typically 3.3 in (85 mm), double column
  6.9 in (175 mm). Design to the width the figure will actually occupy, not to
  your screen.
- **No transparency** in EPS. Some production pipelines still flatten it badly.

## Greyscale first
Journals print in black and white and reviewers print submissions. Every
distinction must survive the loss of colour:
- line style: solid, dashed, dotted
- line weight: hypothesized versus control
- node shape: rectangle for observed, ellipse for latent
- fill: white, light grey, hatched
- direct labels beat a legend, always

## The figure note
Every figure carries a note that lets it be read without the body text:
```
Figure 1. <What the figure shows>. <What each visual element means.>
<Any abbreviation expanded.> <N and time period if relevant.>
<Significance convention if coefficients are shown: * p < .05, ** p < .01,
*** p < .001, two-tailed.>
```
A figure that cannot be understood without the surrounding paragraph will be
asked about in review.

## Numbering and reference
Number in order of first mention. Reference every figure in the text — an
unreferenced figure gets cut in production. APA 7 puts the number and a period
in bold, then the title in italic title case on the next line, then the image,
then the note.

## Accessibility
Do not encode meaning in colour alone (roughly 1 in 12 men has a colour vision
deficiency). If colour is used at all, check the figure in a deuteranopia
simulator. Keep text contrast at or above 4.5:1.

## Chinese-language submissions
图 1, 图 2 numbering with the caption **below** the figure. Body font is
normally 宋体 or 黑体 for labels; keep the label size legible at the printed
column width. 学位论文 templates from the university usually specify the caption
format exactly — follow the template over these defaults.

## The three checks before submitting a figure
1. Print it at actual size, in greyscale. Can you read every label?
2. Does every element in the figure appear in the note?
3. Does the figure agree with the hypotheses in the text, one for one?
