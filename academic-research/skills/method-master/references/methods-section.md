# Methods section templates and reporting standards

## Reporting standard by design
| Design | Standard |
|---|---|
| Randomized trial | CONSORT 2010 (+ CONSORT-SPI for social interventions) |
| Observational quantitative | STROBE |
| Qualitative | COREQ (interviews and focus groups) or SRQR |
| Mixed methods | GRAMMS |
| Systematic review | PRISMA 2020 |
| Scoping review | PRISMA-ScR |
| Qualitative synthesis | ENTREQ |
| Psychology, any | APA JARS-Quant / JARS-Qual / JARS-Mixed |
| Agent-based model | ODD protocol |
| Economics field experiment | AEA RCT Registry entry |

## Quantitative skeleton
```
3. Method
3.1 Data and sample
    Source, frame, collection window with dates, response rate with the AAPOR
    definition used, final analytic n and every exclusion with its count.
3.2 Measures
    One paragraph per construct: definition, items or source, scale, reliability
    (alpha, omega, or test-retest), validity evidence, and the citation for the
    instrument. Full items in an appendix.
3.3 Analytic strategy
    Estimand. Model. Identifying assumption and how it is tested. Standard error
    and clustering level with the reason. Missing data mechanism and treatment
    (MI with m at least as large as the percentage missing, FIML, or listwise
    with a justification). Software and version.
3.4 Robustness
    Pre-specified alternatives, stated as pre-specified or exploratory.
```

## Qualitative skeleton
```
3. Method
3.1 Approach and rationale
    Named tradition, cited, and why it fits the question and the stance.
3.2 Setting and participants
    Recruitment, sampling logic (purposive, theoretical, snowball, maximum
    variation), inclusion criteria, final n with a characteristics table,
    refusals and why.
3.3 Data collection
    Instrument, who conducted it, where, duration, recording, language and any
    translation procedure, field note practice.
3.4 Analysis
    Coding procedure step by step, codebook origin, software, who coded, how
    disagreement was handled, and the stopping rule actually applied.
3.5 Trustworthiness
    Credibility, transferability, dependability, confirmability — each with the
    concrete practice, not the label alone.
3.6 Reflexivity
    Position, relationship to participants, and its plausible effect on the data.
3.7 Ethics
    Approval body and number, consent, anonymization, data handling.
```

## Mixed methods skeleton
Add: the design name and notation, the sequence with a diagram, the point of
integration, the sampling relationship between strands (identical, nested,
parallel), and what a divergence between strands would be taken to mean.

## Sentences that get flagged, and their replacements
| Flagged | Replace with |
|---|---|
| "Data were analyzed using SPSS." | The model, the estimator, the SEs, then the software and version |
| "Themes emerged from the data." | Who coded, from what starting point, by what procedure |
| "Saturation was reached." | The stopping rule, and what actually stopped recruitment |
| "We controlled for relevant variables." | The list, and why each is a confounder and not a mediator or collider |
| "A convenience sample was used." | Who, recruited how, and what the sample cannot support |
| "Reliability was acceptable." | The coefficient, its value, and the threshold applied |
