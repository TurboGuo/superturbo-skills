# Threats to validity — the critique checklist

## Internal validity (did X cause Y)
history · maturation · testing · instrumentation · regression to the mean ·
selection · attrition (differential is the dangerous kind) · diffusion between
conditions · compensatory rivalry · experimenter demand

## External validity (does it travel)
Population: who is in the sample and who is not.
Setting: lab, online panel, one organization, one country.
Time: was the period unusual (a crisis, an election, a pandemic).
Treatment variant: is this treatment the version that exists in the world.
Outcome variant: is this measure the outcome anyone cares about.

## Construct validity (is it the thing)
Underrepresentation · surplus construct irrelevance · mono-operation bias ·
mono-method bias · common method variance (same source, same instrument, same
occasion — test with a marker variable or a CFA marker technique; Harman's
single-factor test is weak and reviewers know it) · social desirability ·
reactivity to being studied.

## Statistical conclusion validity
Low power · violated assumptions · unreliable measures (a reliability of 0.60
attenuates every correlation involving it) · restriction of range · fishing ·
multiple comparisons without correction · heteroskedasticity or clustering
ignored · influential outliers not checked.

## Qualitative trustworthiness (Lincoln & Guba, and what each one needs)
Credibility — prolonged engagement, triangulation, member checking, negative
case analysis.
Transferability — thick description sufficient for the reader to judge fit.
Dependability — an audit trail of analytic decisions.
Confirmability — reflexivity on the researcher's position and its effect.
Note: a reflexive thematic analysis does not owe an inter-rater reliability
statistic, and asking for one misunderstands the method (Braun & Clarke 2019).

## Ethics and governance
IRB or ethics approval and its number · informed consent, and what to do when
consent is impractical · deception and debriefing · vulnerable populations ·
data protection (GDPR, PIPL, institutional policy) · de-identification and
re-identification risk · data sharing plan and its limits · compensation that
is fair without being coercive · dual use and harm to the studied community.

## Reporting and reproducibility
Preregistration and any deviation from it, stated · data and code availability ·
software versions and random seeds · a codebook · CONSORT / STROBE / COREQ /
SRQR / PRISMA / JARS as the field requires.
