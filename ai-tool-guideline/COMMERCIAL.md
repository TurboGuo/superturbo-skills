## Why not a permissive licence

The value is not the list of traps, which anyone can search for. It is the procedure:
the intake that cuts the manual to one person's machine and goal, the three layer
source order that keeps version and price facts off blogs, the graded evidence levels,
the pass check at every stage, and the append protocol that stops the document dying at
the next release. That is what turns a pile of search results into something a client
can follow alone, and it is what they are paying for.

governs where the two differ.
