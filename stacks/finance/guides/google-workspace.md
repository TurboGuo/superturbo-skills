# Google Workspace

**Free with any Google account, 15 GB shared across Drive, Gmail and Photos.**

## What it does here

The evidence layer: contracts, W9 forms, bank statements, the close binder. The 15 GB is shared
across products, and on a finance account it is scanned statements and signed PDFs that consume
it, not mail.

## Install in Claude

Customize > Connectors, connect the Google account once, then toggle Drive, Gmail and Calendar
individually with the **+** button in a chat. On Team and Enterprise an owner enables them at the
organisation level first.

## Install in ChatGPT

Settings > Apps, find Google Drive, Connect. Docs, Sheets and Slides fold into Google Drive
actions rather than appearing as separate apps.
