# Intuit QuickBooks

**Free to connect. Reading a live ledger needs QuickBooks Online, from $38 a month (Simple Start).**

## What it does here

The ledger and the reporting layer. `ar-collections-desk` reads an invoice export; QuickBooks is
where that export comes from, and where the numbers go back to.

## Install in Claude

Customize > Connectors > **+** > Browse connectors > Intuit QuickBooks > Connect.
Then in a chat, **+** > Connectors > toggle it on.
The same connector reaches Intuit Enterprise Suite on a larger plan.

## Install in ChatGPT

Published plugin, so no developer mode and no paid plan. Plugins in the left sidebar, search
Intuit QuickBooks, Connect, sign in.

## Worth checking first

The Claude directory flags this connector authless, while Intuit's help page describes signing in
for the live report path. Those are two halves of the same product. Confirm which half you need
before assuming no subscription is required.
