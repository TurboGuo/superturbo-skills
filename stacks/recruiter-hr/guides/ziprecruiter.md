# ZipRecruiter

**Tier: ADDITIONAL** — connect it for market and comp reference, not as a system of record
**Client cost: $0, and no account at all.** It is **authless** in the Claude directory, the only
connector on this list with nothing to sign up for.

## What it does in the hiring flow

A live reference lookup during intake and at the offer stage: what comparable roles are being
advertised at, right now, in the client's market.

Supports `job-description-writer` when setting a range, and `offer-and-cost-pack` when the
candidate counters.

## Install

claude.ai → **Customize** → **Connect your tools** → search **ZipRecruiter** → enable.
No sign in step. Nothing to configure.

## How widely used

Public company, **NYSE: ZIP**. Q2 2026 revenue **$118.1 million**, **70,721 quarterly paid
employers**, resume database of **over 50 million job seekers**. Real and listed, but small beside
Google, Deel and Calendly.

**Confirmed on both Claude and ChatGPT from a primary source:** its own Q2 2026 shareholder letter
states "Integrations with ChatGPT and Claude to meet job seekers where they are", and says the
ChatGPT integration was deepened in July 2026.

## Honest limit

**One tool, `search_jobs`.** Treat it as a reference lookup. It does not read the client's
pipeline, post jobs, or manage candidates, and it is not an ATS.

## What breaks without it

Nothing. Comp and market questions fall back to web search, which is slower and less structured.
